                                        How to play

                                        1. Tap the kettle to earn rupees. Every tenth glass pays five times.
                                        2. Hire staff in the Kitchen tab. They keep earning while you are away, at reduced speed that improves with franchise milestones.
                                        3. Learn recipes to boost your taps and your income.
                                        4. Your crew draws wages and has morale. In Kitchen, open Team to set pay, hire staff for specific jobs, hire managers, train stalls and handle staff events.
                                        5. The Expand tab unlocks new mechanics: factories, branches, new customers, Night Shift and tea research. Some need a stage or another upgrade first.
                                        6. Serve customers and deliver online orders on the Stall tab. Snacks on your menu make customers pay triple.
                                        7. Finish daily and weekly quests in the Quests tab, then claim the cash.
                                        8. Every hour, the first 10 minutes are Chai Happy Hour with double earnings. Start Rush Hour any time for another double, and claim the daily bonus each day.
                                        9. Once a run reaches ₹50 Arab, open a franchise for a permanent bonus. Franchise milestones unlock bigger perks.
